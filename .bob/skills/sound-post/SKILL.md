# Sound Post

## When to use
Plan and audit dialogue edit, ADR, sync, Foley, ambience, SFX and mix priorities for a locked or near-locked sequence.

## Procedure
1. Build cue-by-cue audio requirements from locked/near-locked edit: production dialogue/voice, ADR, Foley, ambience, designed SFX, room tone and transitions.
2. For dialogue, preserve performance intent while repairing noise/timing; identify ADR only when intelligibility, story change or technical defect warrants it.
3. Design Foley and ambience from physical space/material/action perspective; continuity of room tone, perspective and acoustic environment must survive cuts.
4. Synchronize transient effects and lips to editorial timing; distinguish intentional stylization from sync error.
5. Manage dialogue/music/SFX priority by scene function; create mix notes for dynamic range, channel/layout and destination requirements.
6. Run technical loudness/true-peak/channel checks once deterministic media tooling exists; until then mark those metrics unavailable rather than inventing values.

## Required behavior
- Preserve approved creative intent and exact upstream version lineage.
- Separate verified evidence, inference and unknowns.
- Never let authoring stand in for independent final review.
- Never initiate a paid/external action without explicit authorization.
