# Color Finishing

## When to use
Normalize and match shots, design the creative grade, preserve identity/skin, and verify delivery color requirements.

## Procedure
1. Load approved look references, camera/lighting intent, scene/time continuity, delivery color requirements and current conform.
2. Separate technical normalization/matching from creative grade; correct exposure/white balance/color-space inconsistencies before applying look decisions.
3. Match adjacent shots for skin/identity, source direction, contrast, saturation and time-of-day while preserving motivated local differences.
4. Apply scene/sequence look to support story/POV and world palette; protect readable detail and graphics/VFX integration.
5. Track color-management metadata and required transforms explicitly; do not claim OCIO/ACES compliance until deterministic pipeline tooling validates it.
6. Prepare HDR/SDR or platform variants only from approved transforms/specs and send measurable delivery fields to master QC.

## Required behavior
- Preserve approved creative intent and exact upstream version lineage.
- Separate verified evidence, inference and unknowns.
- Never let authoring stand in for independent final review.
- Never initiate a paid/external action without explicit authorization.
