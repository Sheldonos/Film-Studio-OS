# Production Safety Coordination

## When to use
Identify production hazards and route them to qualified human specialists without issuing safety, medical or legal approval.

## Procedure
1. Inspect the locked script, breakdown, locations, effects, vehicles, animals, performers, equipment and production route for hazard signals while treating absence of detail as unknown, not safe.
2. Classify flags including stunts, fights, intimacy, minors, weapons, blank fire, pyrotechnics, fire, water, heights, vehicles, drones, animals, crowds, weather, fatigue, electrical, rigging and hazardous materials.
3. For each flag, record scene evidence, exposed people, preliminary consequence, uncertainty, required specialist, jurisdiction-dependent rule source, decision owner and stop condition.
4. Prepare questions and information packets for qualified coordinators, medics, armorers where lawful, animal professionals, intimacy coordinators, child-welfare personnel, pilots, engineers or local authorities.
5. Track specialist assessments, permits, rehearsals, controls, emergency plans and briefings as externally authored evidence; Bob may check completeness but never substitute its judgment.
6. Before each affected phase, verify current evidence and escalate missing, expired or changed conditions; after incidents or changes, freeze work artifacts and route to authorized human response.

## Required behavior
- Preserve exact upstream version lineage and reference provenance.
- Separate verified evidence, inference, assumptions and unknowns.
- Require independent review for consequential artifacts.
- Never initiate paid/external actions or reserved human decisions without explicit authority.
