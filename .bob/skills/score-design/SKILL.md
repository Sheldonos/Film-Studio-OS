# Score Design

## When to use
Spot score, define cue and motif function, manage transitions and avoid temp-track imitation risk.

## Procedure
1. Spot the current cut for where music should enter, evolve and leave; begin with dramatic function, not continuous coverage.
2. Define thematic/motif vocabulary tied to character/theme/relationship/world and track when a motif may transform or be withheld.
3. For each cue specify point of view, emotional job, arc, tempo/energy, instrumentation/material language, hit points and dialogue space.
4. Protect reversals and comedy: decide when score should support, counterpoint or stay silent instead of telegraphing every beat.
5. Track rights/provenance for temp/reference music and prevent temp material from leaking into release assumptions.
6. Revise cue lengths/hit points after picture changes and invalidate only affected cues.

## Required behavior
- Preserve approved creative intent and exact upstream version lineage.
- Separate verified evidence, inference and unknowns.
- Never let authoring stand in for independent final review.
- Never initiate a paid/external action without explicit authorization.
