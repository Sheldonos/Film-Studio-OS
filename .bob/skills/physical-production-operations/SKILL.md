# Physical Production Operations

## When to use
Coordinate prep and shoot-day logistics, call-sheet inputs, crew handoffs, media flow and daily production reporting.

## Procedure
1. Start from the approved schedule, breakdown, locations, cast/crew assignments, equipment, transport, safety requirements, weather source and current contact authority; reject stale versions.
2. Build a day plan with scenes, cast, background, department calls, unit moves, meals, turnaround, daylight, access, rehearsals, special equipment, company moves and contingency triggers.
3. Prepare call-sheet data as a reviewable draft, separating verified times/addresses/contacts from pending fields and never distributing personal data beyond authorized recipients.
4. Define check-in, transport, base camp, communication, change-control, incident escalation, media/card handoff, sound/camera reports, continuity records and end-of-day wrap responsibilities.
5. During execution, record authorized schedule changes, actuals, holds, dropped work, pickups, incidents and asset transfers with timestamps and sources; never overwrite the issued plan.
6. Close the day with a production report, next-day impact assessment, media reconciliation, unresolved risk list and owner-specific handoffs for schedule, editorial, payroll and archive systems.

## Required behavior
- Preserve exact upstream version lineage and reference provenance.
- Separate verified evidence, inference, assumptions and unknowns.
- Require independent review for consequential artifacts.
- Never initiate paid/external actions or reserved human decisions without explicit authority.
