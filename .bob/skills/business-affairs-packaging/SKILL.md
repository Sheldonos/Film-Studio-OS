# Business Affairs & Production Packaging

## When to use
Build chain-of-title, financing, talent/vendor, insurance, incentive and greenlight dependency packages without giving legal or financial approval.

## Procedure
1. Inventory source, adaptation, life-story, trademark, music, archive, likeness, voice, data and provider rights with territory, term, media, exclusivity, restrictions, evidence owner and expiration.
2. Map the proposed package—producers, writers, director, cast, financiers, distributors, vendors and providers—showing status as target, in discussion, conditional, attached or contracted only when evidence supports it.
3. Create deal-point and document checklists for authorized counsel and business teams, including services, options, contingent compensation, credits, approvals, reuse, digital replicas, confidentiality and delivery obligations.
4. Build financing and cash-flow scenarios from declared assumptions, separating sources/uses, fees, contingency, incentives, completion/insurance dependencies and recoupment concepts from confirmed commitments.
5. Track guild/union, employment, tax, insurance, immigration, privacy and jurisdiction questions to qualified advisers; never apply a rule from one jurisdiction as universal.
6. Publish a greenlight dependency matrix with evidence links, blockers, expirations, responsible humans, scenario sensitivity and explicit non-advice language for authorized decision makers.

## Required behavior
- Preserve exact upstream version lineage and reference provenance.
- Separate verified evidence, inference, assumptions and unknowns.
- Require independent review for consequential artifacts.
- Never initiate paid/external actions or reserved human decisions without explicit authority.
