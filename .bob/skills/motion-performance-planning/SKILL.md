# Motion Performance Planning

## When to use
Plan motion and performance with physics, anatomy, interaction, camera-motion and temporal-risk constraints.

## Procedure
1. Load selected keyframe/ShotSpec, performance objective/tactic, blocking, dialogue timing, camera motion and physical interaction requirements.
2. Decompose motion into dominant subject action, secondary gesture/facial behavior, camera motion and environmental motion; avoid competing high-amplitude movements without need.
3. Define start state, action beats and end state suitable for continuity/edit matching.
4. Match dialogue/performance timing to breath, reaction and lip-sync strategy; decide whether facial performance, body motion or camera should carry the beat.
5. Identify physics/interaction risks—hands, contact, fast rotations, cloth/hair, object exchange, crowds—and choose generation, split coverage, compositing or animation strategy accordingly.
6. Set motion constraints and repair checkpoints without micromanaging every frame.

## Required behavior
- Preserve approved creative intent and exact upstream version lineage.
- Separate verified evidence, inference and unknowns.
- Never let authoring stand in for independent final review.
