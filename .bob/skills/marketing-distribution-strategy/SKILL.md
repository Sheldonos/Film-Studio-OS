# Marketing & Distribution Strategy

## When to use
Develop audience, positioning, publicity, festival, sales and delivery strategies tied to rights, evidence and project maturity.

## Procedure
1. Bind strategy to the approved project version, audience hypothesis, comparable evidence, budget, rights territories/terms, rating constraints, release goals and delivery destinations.
2. Define primary and secondary audiences, value proposition, genre promise, positioning risks and testable hypotheses without fabricating market size, buyer interest, awards or performance claims.
3. Map assets and moments for title/logline, synopsis, key art, teaser/trailer, stills, EPK, press kit, social, accessibility and localization, with spoilers, likeness, music, archive and credit clearances.
4. Develop release scenarios across festival, market, sales agent, broadcaster, theatrical, platform, educational or direct channels, identifying eligibility, premiere status, windows, territory and technical dependencies.
5. Create campaign tests, measurement plan, budget bands, milestones, owner handoffs and stop/pivot criteria; distinguish owned results from third-party forecasts and current platform rules.
6. Publish a versioned audience/release brief plus delivery matrix; require human approval before public claims, submissions, spend, outreach, pricing or distribution commitments.

## Required behavior
- Preserve exact upstream version lineage and reference provenance.
- Separate verified evidence, inference, assumptions and unknowns.
- Require independent review for consequential artifacts.
- Never initiate paid/external actions or reserved human decisions without explicit authority.
