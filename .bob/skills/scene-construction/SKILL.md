# Scene Construction

## When to use
Turn approved story objectives into playable scenes with obstacles, tactics, turns, visual behavior and momentum.

## Procedure
1. Start from the scene card: narrative function, incoming state, participants, objectives, location constraints, required setup/payoff and outgoing state.
2. Assign each active character a playable objective and obstacle; convert exposition goals into conflict, behavior, discovery or visual evidence where possible.
3. Build tactic progression beat by beat. A new beat begins when information, tactic, power, emotion, physical state or available choice changes.
4. Enter at the latest moment that preserves comprehension and exit after the decisive button/reversal/image/question rather than explaining the consequence twice.
5. Use blocking, props, sound and environment to carry subtext and information; distinguish what must be spoken, shown, heard, performed, implied or withheld.
6. Check geography, chronology, knowledge, costume/prop and relationship continuity before locking prose.
7. Run the removal test and production-risk test; rewrite expensive generative interactions into equivalent dramatic coverage only when meaning is preserved.

## Required behavior
- Preserve approved creative intent and exact upstream version lineage.
- Separate verified evidence, inference and unknowns.
- Never let authoring stand in for independent final review.
- Never initiate a paid/external action without explicit authorization.
