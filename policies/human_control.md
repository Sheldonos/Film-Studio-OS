# Human Control Policy

- **Human-only:** final legal/rights clearance, greenlight, material changes to locked creative invariants, picture lock approval, final master release.
- **Approve-before:** identity replacement, canonical world redesign, major budget/scope change, intentional continuity violations.
- **Review-after:** most bounded writing, planning, generation and repair outputs.
- **Autonomous:** mechanical state tracking, provenance logging, schema validation, staleness propagation, retry of explicitly recoverable failures within attempt budget.

No agent may be both the sole author and sole final approver of a high-risk creative artifact.
